Q: How do I reset my password?
A: Click Forgot Password on the login page and follow the email instructions.

Q: How do I update my email address?
A: Go to Account Settings, click Edit Profile, and update your email. A verification link will be sent.

Q: How do I delete my account?
A: Contact support to permanently delete your account and all associated data.
